## Stage: fit

Measured 2026-09-10 on `CW52D2L07Q`, `os.cpu_count()` = 8, `--once` (one run per pair, so no compile time is separated out).

`NUTSConfig(512, 256, 16)` at tree depth
6, dense mass matrix, on `make_family("aligned10", 0)` at D = 100,
fitted to a scrambled Sobol design observed through `noise_rng(0)` and standardized.
Each (cell, n) is fitted once.
`acq_s` times one `propose_ei` against that fit -- BoTorch's `LogExpectedImprovement`
maximized by `optimize_acqf` at 512 raw Sobol candidates plus as many RAASP
perturbations, 5 L-BFGS-B restarts one at a time, seeded from
`iteration_rngs(0, 0)`; the first run of a pair pays JAX compilation for the fit and torch
compilation for the acquisition, the second pays neither.
`readout_s` times one `readouts(fitted, compute_sobol=True)` call against the same fit --
the full identification readout, Sobol estimator included, which the study computes every
25 iterations and which is therefore a per-run cost of its own.
