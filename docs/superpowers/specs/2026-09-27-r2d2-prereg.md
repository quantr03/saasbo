Design: 2 (structure) × 2 (parameterization) × 2 (prior family: half-Cauchy HC; R2-D2 in form F = "reference", the untied form, at calibration C = C_untied, (a, b, k) = (1.577790731256835, 0.8043916352200708, 0.49942145555129697), α = k·D = 49.942 at D = 100, whose active-count quartiles at D = 100 are 14 / 37 / 61 against the half-Cauchy cells' 17 / 37 / 64; G0) on the eight objective families (`aligned3`, `aligned10`, `anti_aligned`, `decoupled`, `interaction_g0.00/0.25/0.50/0.75`), D = 100, T = 200, the shared 20-point Sobol design, 10 paired seeds. HC runs are the stored ones (launched at a51a4b9 and 2866ed2); R2-D2 runs are new; every cell at 512/256/16, depth 6, one chain, dense mass.

Outcome: final simple regret r = f* - max_{s<=199} f(x_s), y = log10(max(r, 1e-8)).

Effects S, P, Δ_c, ΔS, ΔP as defined in the plan (D11). Smallest effect of interest: 0.3 in log10 regret at T = 200.

Tests: two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank over the 10 seeds within each objective family, Holm within family across effects of one kind; pooled over families, a (family, seed)-cluster bootstrap of the mean (10,000 resamples, 90 % and 95 % intervals).

Predictions:

- PR1 -- wherever S(HC) is significant (Holm p < 0.05), S(R2-D2) has the same sign; likewise P.
- PR2 -- |ΔS| and |ΔP| below 0.3: a 90 % interval inside (-0.3, 0.3) reads "equivalent", entirely outside it "prior family changes the effect", otherwise "inconclusive".
- PR3 (Q6's null) -- |median Δ_c| < 0.3 for every cell and objective family.
- PR4 (secondary) -- AP by native_median and by sobol_hat within 0.05 of the twin's.

Stop rules: the pilot gate G3 as stated in the plan; no interim analysis in stage 4, whose confirmatory analysis runs once all 320 R2-D2 runs are complete; a run failing for a non-statistical reason is resumed, never dropped; NUTS-gate exclusions label iterations and remove no run.

Deviations: a change of form, calibration or budget after launch voids the affected runs, which are rerun and reported.

---

Clarifications of the analysis (Quan, 2026-09-29, before any pilot or stage-4 outcome was read; the text above is unchanged):

- PR1's "same sign" is the sign of the median over seeds of the effect.
- PR4's "within 0.05" is an equivalence reading, parallel to PR2: the 90 % (family, seed)-cluster bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples) of the median paired AP difference (R2-D2 minus its twin) lies inside (-0.05, 0.05) — "equivalent"; entirely outside it — "differs"; otherwise "inconclusive".
- "Holm within family across effects of one kind" uses four kinds within each objective family: {S(HC), P(HC)}, {S(R2-D2), P(R2-D2)}, {ΔS, ΔP}, and the four Δ_c.
