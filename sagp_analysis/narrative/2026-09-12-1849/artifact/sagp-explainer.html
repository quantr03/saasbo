Sparse GP surrogates at D = 100, 200 evaluations

What the sagp study asked, how it tested it and what it found, written for researchers in probabilistic machine learning who know Gaussian processes and Bayesian optimisation but not the high-dimensional BO literature. About ten minutes.

The problem

Bayesian optimisation at D = 100 with a budget of T = 200 noisy evaluations (noise sd 0.1), the first 20 of them a scrambled-Sobol initial design shared by every method within a seed.1 A GP with a full ARD kernel cannot learn 100 lengthscales from 200 points, so high-dimensional BO adds an assumption. The one taken here is low effective dimension along the axes: the objective depends on only 3 to 10 of the 100 coordinates, its active set S, and the surrogate has to discover S while optimising.2 The question is how the surrogate should encode "coordinate i is irrelevant", and whether that choice changes the optimisation, the identification of S, or both.

The idea under test

SAASBO, the established fully Bayesian method, uses a Matérn-5/2 ARD kernel and puts a sparsity-inducing half-Cauchy scale mixture on the inverse squared lengthscales ρi = 1/ℓi2: ρi → 0 stretches coordinate i until it is flat. The alternative under test puts the same global-local prior on per-coordinate amplitudes ai2 of a kernel with one component per coordinate, so that ai2 → 0 silences coordinate i instead. The two parameterisations encode different senses of relevance: an amplitude is a variance share, a lengthscale is a slope.

That choice is crossed with kernel structure. A product of per-coordinate factors can represent interactions but must resolve the joint function on S; a sum of centered per-coordinate components is first-order only, but each component is learned from the projection of all n points onto its coordinate. Two by two gives four cells.

Everything else is held fixed. The prior is SAASBO's own half-Cauchy scale mixture in all four cells, τ2 ~ HC(0.1) with HC(1) factors on the ρ scale, and on the amplitude scale a global scale α = 0.0131 chosen so that both priors imply the same prior-predictive count of active coordinates, plus a LogNormal(0, 1.5) prior on each ℓi.3 The amplitude cells normalise each component to unit average variance, so ai2 is a variance share; the additive-lengthscale cell must stay unnormalised, because there ρi → 0 is what removes a component. Inference is one NUTS chain per refit, 512 warm-up and 256 draws thinned to 16, max_tree_depth 6; a fit is flagged excluded when split-R-hat exceeds 1.1, ESS falls below 16 on any site or more than 5 divergences occur, but its draws are still used to choose the next point.4 The acquisition is analytic LogEI averaged over the retained draws.1

How it was tested

The objectives are generated rather than borrowed, so the ground truth is stated in the units the posteriors are read in: each active coordinate carries a centered one-dimensional GP draw rescaled to an exact variance share, and interaction terms are products of centered draws with their own share. Four families, ten seeds each, with S a random subset per seed:2

  • aligned3, aligned10. 3 or 10 active coordinates with equal shares and ℓ = 0.5. The positive controls, where both parameterisations should agree.
  • decoupled. 8 active coordinates with shares 0.21 ×4 and 0.04 ×4 crossed with ℓ = 1.5 and 0.08, two coordinates per importance-by-roughness class, so that variance share and slope disagree. Built to separate what the two parameterisations track.
  • interaction_g0.25. 5 active coordinates with main-effect shares 0.15 each and two disjoint pairs carrying 0.125 each as product interactions, so a quarter of the variance is beyond any additive model.

Three references: sobol (the initial sequence continued), dsp_map (an ARD Matérn-5/2 at the MAP under the dimension-scaled lengthscale prior of Hvarfner et al., the vanilla baseline) and oracle_S (the same MAP GP restricted to the true S, intended as the ceiling for any variable selection).

Optimisation is scored by simple regret f* − best_f at t = 199. Identification is scored at the last readout in three ways: average precision of ranking the 100 coordinates by the cell's native sparsity parameter (posterior median of ρi or ai2), by sobol_hat (first-order Sobol indices of the posterior mean under the uniform measure, a parameterisation-neutral readout), and by the thresholded rule pactive > 0.5, where pactive is the fraction of the 16 retained draws with share > 0.02 in the amplitude cells or ρ > 0.152, i.e. ℓ < 2.56, in the lengthscale cells.5

Comparisons are paired within seeds because objectives differ by orders of magnitude in attainable regret: on aligned3 a single method's final regret spans 1e-5 to 0.21 across seeds.6 The test is a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank over the 10 seeds. 10/10 wins gives p = 0.002, 9/10 gives 0.021, and 8/10 gives 0.11 with an exact 95 % interval on the win probability that contains a coin flip.7 72 such tests were run, so about 3.6 false positives are expected at p < 0.05 if nothing were real; a Holm column is tabulated but not applied to the headline values.8 The study grades every claim, and this page keeps the grades:9

Supportedat least 9 of 10 seeds and p ≤ 0.01.
Suggestivethe median favours the claim and either p ≤ 0.05 or at least 8 of 10 seeds, but not supported.
Not supportedotherwise. Descriptive claims, with no test, are marked as such.

What was found

1The cell whose kernel structure matches the objective wins, and every cell beats quasi-random search.

Every cell beats sobol in 10 of 10 seeds in every family.10 Supported On the two additive families the additive cells lead. On aligned10, additive-amplitude beats product-lengthscale 10/1011 (medians 0.0273 against 0.289)12 and beats dsp_map and oracle_S 10/10 as well.13 On decoupled, additive-amplitude beats oracle_S and product-lengthscale 10/10 and additive-lengthscale beats product-lengthscale 10/10,14 though additive-amplitude against dsp_map is only 7/10, p = 0.049.15 Suggestive On interaction_g0.25 the order reverses: product-lengthscale 0.000699, product-amplitude 0.00125, oracle_S 0.0119, additive-amplitude 0.0173, additive-lengthscale 0.0214, dsp_map 0.415.16 Product-lengthscale beats dsp_map 10/10,17 but product against additive is 8/10 in all four pairings (p from 0.0098 to 0.065),18 Suggestive, and the additive cells tie oracle_S (p = 0.56).19 On aligned3 every model-based method reaches a median below 0.004 and no cell is distinguishable from dsp_map:20 three active coordinates at this budget do not discriminate surrogate classes.

The parameterisation contrast within a structure is Not supported everywhere except decoupled, where additive-amplitude beats additive-lengthscale 9/10 (p = 0.0137), Suggestive.21

The study's mechanism, an inference rather than a tested claim. Product-lengthscale's own readout sees only about half of S on the additive families (thresholded recall 0.53 on aligned10 and 0.56 on decoupled, median ρ on the true coordinates 0.19 and 0.29 against the 0.152 cutoff):22 an ARD product kernel must resolve the joint 10-dimensional function from 200 points, the SAAS prior shrinks the lengthscales it cannot pin down, and the acquisition optimises the coordinates that remain. The additive kernel learns each component from every point's projection and has no such failure. On interaction_g0.25 the additive posterior mean is the additive projection of f, whose maximiser is not f's; the regret floor of 0.017 to 0.021 is the price.

Take away: the structure main effect, which is the brief's prediction P2. That the additive cells still beat dsp_map twenty-fold in median on interaction_g0.25 says a wrong-but-sparse structural prior is worth more than a right-but-vague one at this D and budget. Do not take away: a parameterisation main effect, or anything about interaction shares other than γ = 0.25, the single point run of the planned sweep.

2All four cells recover S as a ranking, but the thresholded rule under-recalls, partly because its cutoff is applied in design-deflated units.

Ranking works in every cell: pooled average precision by the native posterior median is 0.89 to 0.97 across the four cells (dsp_map 0.677), and by sobol_hat 0.935 to 0.976.23 The median over runs is 1.0 in all four cells.24 Supported (descriptive). The thresholded rule is precise, 0.92 to 1.00 in every cell,25 but conservative: recall is 0.25 (product-amplitude) and 0.51 (additive-amplitude) on aligned10, 0.39 and 0.54 on decoupled,26 against 0.53 and 0.90, then 0.56 and 0.79, for the lengthscale cells.27

Part of that is a units error. The share threshold 0.02 was chosen against the labels, which are variances under the uniform measure of a unit-variance objective, but the cells are fitted to y standardised over the BO design, and a BO design concentrates where f is high: at t = 199 the standardisation std averages 1.52 to 1.56 on aligned10 and 1.33 to 1.40 on decoupled.28 In the model's units a share is therefore divided by about two: a 0.10 share on aligned10 becomes 0.042, and a 0.04 share on decoupled becomes 0.020, the threshold itself.29 On top of that, the posterior medians of ai2 on S sit below even the deflated value, 0.021 (additive-amplitude) and 0.011 (product-amplitude) on aligned10 against 0.042.30 Ranking survives because the on-S values still exceed the largest off-S ai2 by one to two orders of magnitude.31 The fix is offline: rescale ai2 by y_std2 before thresholding.

The decoupled family also shows what the two parameters measure. The lengthscale cells' native score orders by roughness, as its units dictate: additive-lengthscale's median ρ is 120 on the high-share rough class against 2.2 on the high-share smooth class at the same true share.32 The amplitude cells order by importance with roughness leaking in: additive-amplitude's median ai2 is 0.079 on high-rough against 0.038 on high-smooth.33 The neutral readout does what it was built for: sobol_hat's medians for additive-amplitude are 0.23 and 0.20 on the two high-share classes and 0.037 and 0.032 on the low-share ones, against true shares 0.21 and 0.04.34 Supported (descriptive), and sobol_hat is the readout to report as a share estimate.

Take away: ranking by either readout, and sobol_hat as the share estimate. Do not take away: recall or F1 at pactive > 0.5 as a property of the cells; the threshold is in the wrong units and, in the amplitude cells, pactive is a sampler artefact (finding 3).

3The amplitude cells' NUTS chains never passed the convergence gate, so their posteriors are not to be trusted, even though their optimisation results are.

Gate exclusion is 100 % in both amplitude cells, 7,200 of 7,200 iterations each, with r_hat_max median 1.49 and 1.51 and n_eff_min median 6.1 and 5.9 against thresholds of 1.1 and 16.35 The failing sites are the amplitudes and lengthscales (median R-hat 1.40 to 1.45, ESS 6.5 to 7); the global sites pass (R-hat 1.04 to 1.05, ESS 36 to 38).36 The lengthscale cells fail marginally, in 52.8 % and 59.3 % of fits.37 NUTS reaches max_tree_depth in 95.3 to 100 % of fits in every cell, so every trajectory is truncated.38 The structural reason the amplitude cells are worse is that they sample 2D + 3 = 203 sites against D + 4 = 104;39 the funnel of the amplitude scale mixture at α = 0.013 is the study's hypothesis for the geometric one. Not supported: that the amplitude cells' posteriors are converged.

What survives. Regret does, because the loop queried with the draws it had; an unconverged posterior is a worse surrogate, so the amplitude cells are handicapped rather than favoured. Ranking by the native median survives as an empirical fact, since it needs only the order across coordinates. pactive does not: it is a fraction of 16 draws from about 6 effective ones, and on the true aligned10 coordinates its 10th to 90th percentile range is 0.00 to 0.94.40 Where both kinds of fit exist, in the lengthscale cells, non-convergence does cost identification: at t ≥ 100 passing fits have mean AP 0.90 and 0.94 against 0.83 and 0.79 for excluded ones, and the amplitude cells' excluded-only means of 0.85 and 0.88 sit in the same band.41

Take away: the regret comparisons and the rankings. Do not take away: pactive, any calibration statement, or any per-seed readout from the amplitude cells until the sampler pilot below has run.

4Two things look wrong: oracle_S is a weak reference, and additive-lengthscale flips into an all-active posterior mode after it has converged.

oracle_S is weak. It loses to additive-amplitude 10/10 on aligned10 (median 0.43 against 0.027) and on decoupled (0.44 against 0.058), and 9/10 to additive-lengthscale on both;42 on interaction_g0.25 it is third, tied with the additive cells.43 Supported, but as a statement about the reference. The reason is inductive bias: the oracle knows S but fits one ARD Matérn-5/2 GP at the MAP on it, a full-interaction model that cannot pool information across coordinates on exactly the families where the additive cells can. It also wastes queries: 24, 12, 8 and 10 % of its late queries, by family, are near-copies of an earlier query, against 0 to 1 % for the cells and dsp_map;44 the study's hypothesis is that an acquisition exactly flat in the 97 inactive coordinates lets the optimiser's perturbations of the incumbent beat its raw candidates. A fair oracle would share the structural class of the cell under test, or be the cell itself with the prior restricted to S.

The all-active mode. On aligned3, additive-lengthscale declares all 100 coordinates active in five of ten seeds, in 25, 23, 27, 70 and 54 of their 180 readouts, never before t = 73,45 and intermittently: seed 02 alternates between 100 and 3 active across its late readouts.46 The retained draws say what it is: in the sparse mode the outputscale is 0.48 with ρ 2.3 on S and 7e-5 off S;47 in the collapsed mode all 16 draws have 98 to 100 coordinates above the cutoff, the outputscale is 0.0034, τ2 is 82 and ρ is 167 on S and 121 off S.48 A second posterior mode, in which every coordinate carries a low-amplitude, short-lengthscale component and the global scale absorbs the difference. It begins only after the run has converged, seed 08's regret having been 0.000019 since t = 60,49 so regret is unaffected. Single-chain R-hat is blind to it: six of seven fully collapsed readouts pass the gate with r_hat_max 1.04 to 1.10,50 and only the transitions are caught.51 sobol_hat is right in both modes, AP 1.00 and 0.92 in the two flagged seeds against 0.02 and 0.04 by ρ.52 The study's hypothesis, medium confidence: the additive-lengthscale kernel is deliberately unnormalised, so ℓ → 0 gives a nearly white additive term that trades against the noise site; the amplitude cells carry a LogNormal ℓ prior put there to guard exactly this corner, and the lengthscale cells have none.53

Take away: score identification by sobol_hat or by a readout pooled over several iterations, never by the last ρ readout; and the oracle rows currently mislead. Do not take away: any effect on regret.

What it means, and what comes next

At D = 100 and T = 200, kernel structure matters more than where the sparsity prior sits; a sparse prior with the wrong structure beats a well-scaled dense prior with the right one; and the rankings are trustworthy while the thresholded readouts and the amplitude posteriors are not. The study's next steps, ordered by information per GPU-hour:54

  1. Re-score the existing readouts, no GPU: apply the share threshold in the right units and score identification by sobol_hat and by a readout pooled over the last k iterations. Minutes of CPU.
  2. A sampler pilot on the saved designs, about 1 to 5 GPU-hours: for the amplitude cells, max_tree_depth 8 and 10, 4 chains × 256 draws, and a log-scale or non-centered reparameterisation of the amplitude scale mixture; for additive-lengthscale on aligned3, 4 chains, to make the two modes visible in R-hat. A fit takes 38 to 58 s.55
  3. A fair oracle, about 10 CPU-hours: additive-structured on S for the additive families, product-structured for interaction_g0.25.

Further out: ten more seeds on decoupled and interaction_g0.25, where verdicts are suggestive, and the families not yet run.

Scope. Only aligned3, aligned10, decoupled and interaction_g0.25 were run. The brief also planned anti_aligned, a rotated family, the γ sweep, dense_weak, embedded Hartmann-6, Lasso-DNA and MOPTA08; none of those ran. The four cells totalled 1,944 GPU-hours, 1,584 of them in the acquisition,56 because the centered cells' kernels loop over the 100 coordinates: 182 to 471 s per iteration against 8 to 11 s for product-lengthscale.57

Terms as this study uses them twelve entries
SAAS prior
Sparse axis-aligned subspace: a half-Cauchy scale mixture, global scale times per-coordinate scales, that shrinks most parameters to zero and lets a few escape.
ρi
The inverse squared lengthscale 1/ℓi2; the lengthscale cells' native sparsity parameter, larger meaning rougher and more relevant.
ai2
The amplitude of coordinate i's component after normalisation to unit average variance, so that it equals the component's variance share.
Variance share
The fraction of the objective's variance under the uniform measure carried by one coordinate: its first-order Sobol index; the labels the families are generated with.
Additive kernel
A sum of one-dimensional centered Matérn-5/2 components, one per coordinate; first-order effects only.
Native score
The posterior median of the cell's own sparsity parameter, ρi or ai2, used to rank coordinates.
sobol_hat
First-order Sobol indices of the posterior mean under the uniform measure; the parameterisation-neutral readout.
pactive
The fraction of the 16 retained draws in which coordinate i clears the cutoff, share > 0.02 or ρ > 0.152; a coordinate is declared active when it exceeds 0.5.
The gate
A fit is excluded when split-R-hat > 1.1 or ESS < 16 on any sampled site, or more than 5 divergences; excluded draws are still used by the loop.
Simple regret
f* minus the best objective value queried so far, on the noiseless objective.
dsp_map
An ARD Matérn-5/2 GP at the MAP under a lengthscale prior scaled with dimension; the vanilla high-dimensional baseline.
oracle_S
The same MAP GP given the true active set through a feature filter, so the acquisition still searches all 100 coordinates.
