# Draft figure captions

Figures are in `/scratch/work/tranq8/sagp_analysis/latest/figures/`. Numbers in the captions come from the tables cited in `claims_ledger.csv`; the captions are written to stand on their own in a results section.

**Figure 1. `regret_curves.png`. Median simple regret against iteration.**
Median over 10 seeds of the regret f* − best_f (log scale, floored at 1e-6) at each BO iteration t = 20 to 199, with the interquartile band, for the four surrogate cells (product / additive structure, lengthscale / amplitude sparsity), the quasi-random reference (sobol), the dimension-scaled-prior MAP GP (dsp_map) and the oracle restricted to the true coordinates (oracle_S); one panel per objective family, D = 100, 20-point shared initial design. The cell whose structure matches the objective leads from the middle of the run: on aligned10 and decoupled the additive cells separate from the product cells and both references by t = 100 (additive-amplitude medians 0.18 and 0.20 against 0.48 and 0.54 for product-lengthscale), and on interaction_g0.25 the product cells pull ahead after t = 125. On aligned3 every model-based method is below 0.004 by t = 199 and the curves are indistinguishable. Bands are wide because seeds differ by orders of magnitude in attainable regret; comparisons are made within seeds (Figure 2, section 2).

**Figure 2. `final_regret_strip.png`. Final regret per seed at t = 199.**
Each dot is one seed's final regret for one method (log scale), the bar the median over the 10 seeds; one panel per family; seeds are paired across methods. Every cell beats sobol in 10 of 10 seeds in every family. On aligned10 additive-amplitude beats dsp_map, oracle_S and product-lengthscale in 10 of 10 seeds; on interaction_g0.25 product-lengthscale beats dsp_map in 10 of 10 while the additive cells tie oracle_S. Three seeds deserve a look: interaction_g0.25 seed 08, where three cells and dsp_map stop 2.4 below f* and product-lengthscale and oracle_S reach 0.013; aligned3 seeds 01 and 08, where product-lengthscale and oracle_S share an identical regret because both stayed at a boundary vertex found at t = 20; and decoupled seed 04, hard for every method. Dots at the 1e-6 floor reached smaller regret.

**Figure 3. `identification.png`. Active-set identification at the last readout.**
Per seed and cell, three scores of how well the fit at t = 199 identifies the true active set S: average precision of ranking the 100 coordinates by the cell's native sparsity parameter (rho_i for the lengthscale cells, a_sq_i for the amplitude cells; left), by the first-order Sobol indices of the posterior mean (sobol_hat; centre), and F1 of the thresholded rule p_active > 0.5 (right); bars are medians; dsp_map is shown for reference and sobol has no model. Ranking works in every cell (median AP 1.0), with the two additive-lengthscale aligned3 seeds at AP 0.02 and 0.04 on the left panel being the all-active mode of section 5a, which sobol_hat (centre) does not see. The right panel shows the amplitude cells' low recall on aligned10 and decoupled (F1 medians 0.40 to 0.67), where equal 0.1 shares and 0.04 shares fall at or below the 0.02 share threshold once the design-standardization is accounted for. dsp_map's low F1 is a precision problem: it declares 8 to 24 coordinates active.

**Figure 4. `identification_vs_t.png`. Identification quality over the run.**
Median over seeds of AP by native_median (top row) and of F1 at p_active > 0.5 (bottom row) at the readouts t = 20, 25, 50, ..., 175, 199, per family (columns) and cell, with dsp_map dashed. The structure-matched cell locks on first: all cells reach AP 1.0 at t = 50 on aligned3; additive-lengthscale reaches AP 0.9 at t = 125 on aligned10 and t = 150 on decoupled, the other cells 50 to 75 iterations later; product-lengthscale reaches AP 1.0 at t = 125 on interaction_g0.25 where additive-lengthscale peaks at 0.89 and declines to 0.78 by t = 199 as a spurious coordinate climbs the ranking. F1 saturates below 0.8 for the amplitude cells on aligned10 and decoupled and is still rising at t = 199, so 200 evaluations is not a saturated identification budget there.

**Figure 5. `diagnostics.png`. NUTS convergence diagnostics over all 7,200 iterations per cell.**
Left: histogram of the maximum split-R-hat over sampled sites per fit (clipped at 3; gate at 1.1). Centre: histogram of log10 of the minimum effective sample size (gate at 16). Right: fraction of fits failing the gate against iteration (bins of 20). The two amplitude cells fail in 100 % of fits, with r_hat_max concentrated around 1.5 and ESS around 6, on the a_sq and kernel_ell sites while the global sites pass; the lengthscale cells fail marginally in 53 % and 59 % of fits, peaking at 70 to 75 % around t = 60 to 80. Not shown: NUTS reaches max_tree_depth = 6 in 95 to 100 % of fits in every cell, so every trajectory is truncated. Gate failure does not stop a run: the draws are used regardless, so regret is valid for all runs while the readouts of failing fits are less trustworthy.

**Figure 6. `cost_vs_t.png`. Per-iteration wall time of the fit and of the acquisition, by cell and GPU.**
Median fit time (left) and acquisition-maximization time (right) per iteration in bins of 10, for the four cells, split by the GPU that ran the iteration (H200 solid, H100 dashed, A100 dotted); the references ran on CPU and are not shown. Times are comparable only within one GPU type. Fits cost 30 to 75 s and grow with n in every cell; the acquisition is what separates the cells: 8 to 11 s for product-lengthscale (BoTorch's own SAAS kernel) against 180 to 470 s for the three centered cells, whose torch kernels loop over the 100 coordinates. The four cells totalled about 1,944 GPU-hours of fit plus acquisition, 788 of them for additive-amplitude, whose slowest runs exceeded the 24 h job limit and were resumed.
